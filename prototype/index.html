Who we are

Rooted in Cameroon, built on six decades

Longhorn Publishers Cameroon Ltd is the Central African arm of Longhorn Publishers PLC — a publishing house with sixty years of experience creating educational and general-interest content across the continent.

From our office in Tsinga, Yaoundé, we operate as content creators and platform business providers: an editorial, creative and production team working in both official languages, close enough to the market to get the cultural detail right.

Our process

How we work, start to finish

Five stages, each with a named owner and a sign-off before the next begins.

Step 01

Consultation

We map the scope, audience, curriculum requirements and language needs before quoting.

Step 02

Planning

Schedule, team allocation and milestones agreed in writing, with both language tracks planned together.

Step 03

Execution

Editorial, design and illustration run in parallel against the agreed brief.

Step 04

Quality Review

Proofreading, factual checks and curriculum alignment verified before sign-off to print.

Step 05

Delivery

Printing, finishing and distribution to the schedule set at planning.
