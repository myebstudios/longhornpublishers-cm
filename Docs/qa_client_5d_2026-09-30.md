### Findings

- **F1 (P3, local only, predates this work):** `src/pages/api/catalogue.ts` calls `getDatabase()` with no connection string, while pages use `src/lib/build-database.ts`, which honours `CMS_DATABASE_URL`. With `CMS_DATABASE_URL` set, pages read Postgres and the API reads PGlite, so the two can disagree about which titles exist (or the API 500s, as in S1). Production isn't affected, because both resolve to Netlify DB. Fix: use the same helper in the API route.
- **F2 (P4):** in S6, "migrations not applied. Stop it and rerun" is printed even when the running server is healthy and up to date. Wording only.
- **F3 (P4, known):** dev-only sample slides render three `<h1>`s on `/en/` locally. They never render in production, but they'll inflate slide counts in local slide QA. Part 2 will count only non-demo rows.

### Findings (not blocking)

- **F4 (P3, already live on every page, predates CLIENT-5):** the footer `mailto:longhorncameroon@longhornpublishers.com` link can't wrap in its 191 px column. It causes horizontal scroll of 73 px at 1024, 40 px at 1180 and 18 px at 1280. It's fine at ≥1366 px, which is why CLIENT-3D's 1440 px check missed it. Reproduced on `longhornpublishers-cm.netlify.app` EN/FR. Fix: `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on footer contact links.
- **F5 (P3, regression at 320×568 only):** the carousel stacks its slides, so the tallest slide sets the height for all of them. At 320 px, the FR LoHo headline takes 6 lines, the hero grows to 793 px, and **every FR slide's CTA sits 65 px below the fold** on 320×568. It's fine at 375×667 and 414×896, and in EN at every size. Live today it's at 531 px, above the fold. Options: a smaller `h1` below 360 px, or accept it.
- **F6 (P4, cosmetic, for Z):** on slide 4 the em dash leads the second line ("— Coming soon", "— Bientôt disponible") at EN 375/414/768/1280 and FR 320. That's because "LoHo e-learning —" doesn't fit the headline measure. Either drop the dash (the accent's style already separates the two parts) or accept it.
- **F7 (P4, local tooling):** `stage-hero-launch.mjs` keeps a single `.netlify/hero-launch-snapshot.json` regardless of `LOCAL_DB_PORT`, and `--rollback` reports "restored N prior slides" without checking `rowCount`. A snapshot from one DB rolled back against another updated 0 rows and still printed "restored 1". Key the snapshot by DB URL and fail if `rowCount` is 0.
- **F8 (P4, local hygiene):** the shared checkout's `.netlify/blobs-serve` hero blobs are from 2026-09-29 (the old HERO-7 images, and `07e4` is the print-inspection photo). No running server renders them today (4321 shows dev sample slides, 4391 is down), but restage before any visual review on that checkout.
- **F9 (P4, after release):** `verify:cms-parity` asserts that slide 1 equals the legacy hero. After the swap (slide 1 CTA becomes "Explore our services" → `/services`) it will fail by design. Update its expectation with the release. The runbook doesn't use it.
- **Withdrawn:** a `verify:cms-parity` failure I first saw came from my own PGlite staging leaking into the fallback server. On a clean store it passes (Q11).
