## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

This proposal covers the design and development of a bespoke, standalone corporate website for Longhorn Publishers Cameroon Ltd, plus a custom admin panel for ongoing content management. The site is built entirely from the Longhorn Company Profile (Sep 2026) and does not mirror the Kenyan HQ site (longhornpublishers.com) in layout or content, giving the Cameroon entity its own distinct, modern digital identity while retaining the credibility of Longhorn Publishers PLC's 60+ year heritage.

The platform is bilingual (English/French) from the ground up, reflecting Cameroon's dual-language education system, and covers both sides of the business: the finished learning materials Longhorn Cameroon publishes (Catalogue) and the editorial/production services it offers to other organisations (Publishing Services).

Visual direction: authoritative and grounded (deep burgundy/maroon), balanced with a sense of growth and approachability (vibrant leaf green), clean geometric typography, and authentic imagery of the Yaoundé team, editorial process, and printing production — moving away from generic stock textbook photography.

## 2. CONFIRMED DELIVERABLES

### 2.1 Public Website (9 Pages, Bilingual EN/FR)

- **Home** — Hero, "Who We Are," Services preview, Catalogue preview, "One Partner, End-to-End Solution" banner, trust indicators, Latest Updates, closing CTA.
- **About Us** — Heritage & local presence, Purpose/Vision/Mission/Values, Team & Capacity.
- **Publishing Services** — Editorial, Creative, and Production service breakdown; the 5-Step Process timeline.
- **Catalogue** — Filterable grid (Level, Subject, Language) of primary/secondary learning materials, with individual title detail pages.
- **Why Choose Us** — Local presence, quality commitment, proof points.
- **Contact Us** — Address, phone, email, branded map, and a B2B inquiry form with project-type selection.
- **News & Updates** — Category-filterable article list (Company News, New Titles, Partnerships, Events) with individual article pages.
- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use** — footer-linked legal pages.

Every page is served under bilingual sub-path routing (`/en/`, `/fr/`) with a persistent language toggle, `hreflang` tags, and localised French URL slugs.

### 2.2 Custom Admin Panel

A purpose-built, password-protected admin panel at `/admin`, giving the Longhorn Cameroon team full control over the site without developer involvement post-launch:

- Email + password login (invite-only access)
- Site Settings (address, phone, email, social links, footer content) — edit once, updates sitewide
- Homepage content editor
- About Us, Why Choose Us, and Contact page editors
- Publishing Services list manager (add/edit/reorder services and process steps)
- Catalogue manager — add/edit titles, cover image upload, level/subject/language tagging, draft/publish control
- News & Updates manager — add/edit articles, image upload, category tagging, draft/publish control
- Legal pages editor
- Bilingual fields throughout (English and French side by side, with validation before publishing)

### 2.3 Technical Foundation

- Astro + Tailwind CSS frontend, statically rendered for speed and SEO
- Managed Postgres database and file storage for all content and media
- Local SEO setup: structured data (Organisation, LocalBusiness, Service schemas), meta tags, sitemap
- Deployment to production hosting under the client's domain

## 3. TECHNICAL DIRECTION NOTES

- All copy and structure are sourced strictly from the Longhorn Company Profile (Sep 2026) — no HQ content is reused beyond a single credit link to the parent company.
- The site must clearly distinguish "Catalogue" (what Longhorn Cameroon publishes) from "Publishing Services" (what they do for other organisations) throughout the copy, so B2B partners and institutional buyers aren't confused about which offering applies to them.
- The Longhorn Cameroon team must provide final catalogue title data (covers, descriptions, curriculum alignment), legal page content (Privacy Policy, Terms of Use), and any additional imagery; the site can launch with a smaller curated catalogue and scale via the admin panel.
