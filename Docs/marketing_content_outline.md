### Our Editorial & Publishing Services
A comprehensive, full-service capability showcasing:
- **Editing:** Improving the accuracy of language, flow, organization, structure, and overall readability.
- **Proofreading:** Comparing proofs against original manuscripts/graphics to identify transcription errors.
- **Translation:** Rephrasing content to English or French—essential for bilingual educational environments.
- **Designing:** Arranging text, formatting, and visual assets to elevate the manuscript and cover design.
- **Illustration:** Using classic fine arts to emphasize the communication of ideas.
- **Printing:** Delivering high-quality printing of texts and images.

### Our Catalogue
- **Headline:** "Our Learning Materials"
- **Body:** Showcasing primary and secondary learning and teaching materials developed for the National Curriculum in Cameroon and the DRC, available in both English and French.
- **Structure:** Browsable by Level (Primary/Secondary), Subject, and Language, with a detail view per title covering curriculum alignment and a "Request This Title" inquiry path.
- **Distinction from Services:** This section showcases *what Longhorn Cameroon publishes* (finished titles); "Publishing Services" covers *what they do for other organizations* (editorial/production work-for-hire).
