## 3. Kenya-Only Features & Cameroon Market Caveats

Deploying LoHo in Cameroon requires strict geographical and pedagogical localization. The following table identifies features that are **Kenya-only** and must **never** be cited or implied on the Cameroon platform:

| Feature / Dimension | Kenya Reality (Official Platform) | Cameroon Caveat & Safe Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| **Curriculum Framework** | Aligned to Kenya's **Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC)** and 8-4-4 system (Grade 1 to Form 4), accredited by **KICD** (Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development). | **Cameroon has its own educational systems.** Cameroon operates dual sub-systems (Francophone and Anglophone) under the Ministry of Basic Education (MINEDUB) and Ministry of Secondary Education (MINESEC). LoHo cannot claim CBC or KICD approval in Cameroon. It must be described as adaptable, curriculum-relevant digital learning. |
| **Sub-Brand "Elimu Pepe"** | Branded in Kenya as **Elimu Pepe** ("Instant Education" in Swahili), with dedicated Swahili revision courses (*SOMO Kiswahili*). | **Swahili is not an official language in Cameroon.** Official languages are English and French. The sub-brand "Elimu Pepe" and Swahili titles must not appear. The product in Cameroon is simply **LoHo** or **LoHo Learning**. |
| **Connectivity & Telco Partnerships** | Integrated with **Safaricom PLC**, featuring Safaricom-subsidized tablets, SIM bundles, and **M-PESA** automated payments. | **Safaricom and M-PESA do not operate in Cameroon.** Cameroon's telco and mobile money landscape is dominated by MTN MoMo, Orange Money, and Camtel. No marketing or checkout copy may mention Safaricom or M-PESA. |
| **Commercial Status** | Live commercial product in schools and retail in Kenya. | **"Coming soon" in Cameroon.** The client DOCX explicitly states: `E-learning product LoHo (coming soon)`. It must remain marked with a non-clickable status badge: **Coming soon** (EN) / **Bientôt disponible** (FR). |
| **Hardware Bundles** | Packaged learning tablets with filtered MDM internet access. | Until hardware partnerships in Cameroon are officially contracted, marketing should focus on the **software platform / digital content**, not physical tablet bundles. |
