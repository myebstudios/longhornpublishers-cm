## 3. Kenya-Only Features & Cameroon Market Exclusions

Deploying LoHo in Cameroon requires strict geographical and pedagogical boundaries. The following features are **Kenya-only** and are **strictly excluded** from Cameroon customer communications:

| Feature / Dimension | Kenya Reality (Official Platform) | Cameroon Exclusion & Safe Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| **Curriculum Framework** | Aligned to Kenya's **Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC)** and 8-4-4 system (Grade 1 to Form 4), accredited by **KICD** (Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development). | **Cameroon has its own educational systems.** Cameroon operates dual sub-systems (Francophone and Anglophone) under MINEDUB and MINESEC. LoHo cannot claim CBC or KICD approval in Cameroon. |
| **Digital Assessments & Quizzes** | Automated continuous assessment tests, digital quizzes, and exam analytics. | **Kenya-only feature.** No digital assessments may be promised, marketed, or implied for Cameroon. |
| **Classroom Deployment & LMS** | Turnkey school LMS infrastructure, connected classroom hardware, and live school deployments. | **Kenya-only feature.** No live classroom deployment, school tracking infrastructure, or institutional integration may be claimed in Cameroon. |
| **Sub-Brand "Elimu Pepe"** | Branded in Kenya as **Elimu Pepe** ("Instant Education" in Swahili), with dedicated Swahili courses (*SOMO Kiswahili*). | **Swahili is not an official language in Cameroon.** Official languages are English and French. The sub-brand "Elimu Pepe" and Swahili titles must not appear. The product in Cameroon is simply **LoHo**. |
| **Telco & Payments** | Integrated with **Safaricom PLC**, featuring subsidized tablets, SIM bundles, and **M-PESA** automated payments. | **Safaricom and M-PESA do not operate in Cameroon.** Cameroon's mobile payments landscape is dominated by MTN MoMo, Orange Money, and Camtel. Marketing and billing copy must omit Safaricom and M-PESA. |
| **Commercial Status** | Live commercial product in schools and retail in Kenya. | **"Coming soon" in Cameroon.** The client DOCX explicitly states: `E-learning product LoHo (coming soon)`. Must remain badged as **Coming soon** (EN) / **Bientôt disponible** (FR). No early access, pre-orders, or immediate availability may be promised. |
