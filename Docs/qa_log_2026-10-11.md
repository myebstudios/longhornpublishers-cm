### Failures and defects (fix before closing)

- **F1 — WCAG 1.4.3 contrast fail (blocker for AA).** Slide eyebrow, white on `rgb(244,126,39)`, is 2.67:1 at 13.2px/600 uppercase (needs 4.5:1). It appears on slides 2–4 in EN and FR at every viewport. Candidate fix: dark text (burgundy-deep `rgb(59,15,27)` is 6.19:1 on this orange) or a darker pill. `.eyebrow` is global, so check other uses.
- **F2 — 320×568 first viewport.** Mobile hero `min-height: clamp(640px, 90vh, 780px)` exceeds 568px. FR: CTA at y 575–633 is fully below the fold on all slides. EN slide 1: CTA at y 518–577 is cut by the fold. Carousel controls at y 623–713 are below the fold on every slide. Fine at 320×640 and larger.
- **F3 — Touch swipe does not work.** The stack has `touch-action: auto`, so the browser fires `pointercancel` during a horizontal swipe and `pointerup` never arrives (events seen: `pointerdown, touchstart, pointercancel, touchend`). The handler works when pointer events are dispatched synthetically. Candidate fix: `touch-action: pan-y` on `[data-hero-stack]`. Confirmed in emulated Chromium touch only.
- **F4 — Play can look dead.** With focus on the toggle, pressing Play (click or Enter) sets the label to "Pause slideshow" and `aria-pressed=false`, but nothing advances: `data-progress-paused=true` because focus-in pauses autoplay. Slides advance only after focus leaves the carousel (blur + 7s: slide 2). Confirmed in EN and FR. Suggest not treating focus on the toggle itself as a pause reason.
- **F5 — Slide 3 crop at ≤414px.** The subject's face is cut off (centered `object-position`, no mobile focal point). Slides 1, 2 and 4 crop acceptably. Art-direction judgement call.
